A hundred days to build the stage under you.

Four tracks, paced across 100 days — acting, content, networking, and the day job that pays for all of it.

The order below runs beginner to advanced within each track. You don't need to finish one track before starting the next — networking and the day-job reading can run underneath the acting and content work the whole time. Roughly one book every 5–6 days keeps pace with the 100-day window; skip around if something's clearly more useful to you right now.

I

Finding your footing

Days 1–25 · the craft and business of acting
  • Audition — Michael Shurtleff Beginner
    How auditions actually get decided, from the casting side of the table.
  • Respect for Acting — Uta Hagen Beginner
    The foundational technique book most working actors still return to.
  • An Actor Prepares — Constantin Stanislavski Intermediate
    Where modern naturalistic acting technique started — worth reading once, slowly.
  • True and False — David Mamet Intermediate
    A blunt corrective to acting-school mysticism, from someone who's directed a lot of actors.
  • Self-Management for Actors — Bonnie Gillespie Advanced
    Treats your acting career as a small business you run yourself — submissions, marketing, all of it.
II

Building your stage

Days 26–50 · YouTube and content creation
  • Crushing It! — Gary Vaynerchuk Beginner
    A framework for building a personal brand out of what you're already doing.
  • YouTube Secrets — Sean Cannell & Benji Travis Beginner
    The nuts and bolts — thumbnails, retention, upload schedules — from two people who've done it.
  • Videocracy — Kevin Allocca Intermediate
    Why videos actually spread, from someone who watched it happen from inside YouTube.
  • Content Inc. — Joe Pulizzi Intermediate
    Build the audience first, figure out what to sell them second.
  • Company of One — Paul Jarvis Advanced
    The case for staying small and sustainable instead of chasing scale for its own sake.
III

Working the room

Days 51–75 · networking
  • How to Win Friends and Influence People — Dale Carnegie Beginner
    Dated in places, but the core idea — genuine interest in people — still holds up.
  • Never Eat Alone — Keith Ferrazzi Intermediate
    Networking reframed as generosity and follow-through, not transactional schmoozing.
  • The Like Switch — Jack Schafer & Marvin Karlins Intermediate
    An ex-FBI behavioral analyst's read on what actually makes people like you.
  • Give and Take — Adam Grant Advanced
    The data-backed case that givers win in the long run — with real caveats about burnout.
IV

The day job

Days 76–100 · corporate and remote work
  • Deep Work — Cal Newport Beginner
    The focus skills that make remote work actually productive instead of just flexible.
  • Remote: Office Not Required — Jason Fried & David Heinemeier Hansson Intermediate
    Written by people who built a fully remote company before it was normal.
  • The Trusted Advisor — David Maister, Charles Green & Robert Galford Advanced
    How to earn trust quickly — useful for freelance work and corporate relationships alike.
  • So Good They Can't Ignore You — Cal Newport Advanced
    Skill-building beats "follow your passion" — a fitting close to the 100 days.
